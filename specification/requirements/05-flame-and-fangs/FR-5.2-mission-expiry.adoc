=== FR-5.2 Mission expiry

Every mission dialog except the boss shows "Intel Expires In HH:MM:SS" in dark text above the
"View" button.

.Acceptance criteria
. The expiry is read without an OCR engine, by digit templates for the dark text.
. `ref/combat-1-dialog.png` reads 13:51:07, `ref/hunt-1-dialog.png` 13:48:42,
  `ref/hunt-1-dialog-banner.png` 14:59:41 and `ref/rescue-1-dialog-banner.png` 14:59:19.
. `ref/boss-1-dialog.png` has no expiry; the result is "none", never a guessed time.
. A glyph that cannot be classified makes the whole expiry "unreadable".
. The expiry is only read while the event icon is shown (<<FR-5.1>>).
