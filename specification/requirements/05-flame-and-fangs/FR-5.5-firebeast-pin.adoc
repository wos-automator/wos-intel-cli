=== FR-5.5 Firebeast pin

During the event a gold pin with the head of the firebeast appears on the Intel screen. It is a
hunt and runs in the hunt flow (SPEC.md §3.7).

.Acceptance criteria
. On `ref/flame-and-fangs-missions.png` the firebeast pin is found as a `HUNT` of tier `GOLD`
  at 430,279 (±6 px), next to the other symbols of that screenshot.
. Its status follows <<FR-2.3>> like every other pin.
. Its mask scores below the threshold of 0.75 on every other reference screenshot.
