[[FR-5.4]]
=== FR-5.4 Expert before the event

.Acceptance criteria
. While the event icon is shown with a countdown above 2 h (configurable), the expert sign is
  not tapped; it is still logged (<<FR-3.8>>).
. With a countdown of 2 h or less the sign is tapped by the rule of <<FR-3.8>>, in which
  missions of another group than the current phase count as not workable (<<FR-5.3>>).
. The missions the expert hands out expire after the event start and are ignored.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.6, §3.7 +
Effort:: 1 h
