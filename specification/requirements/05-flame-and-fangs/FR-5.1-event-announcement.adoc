=== FR-5.1 Event announcement and countdown

The day before the event an icon in the lower right corner of the Intel screen counts down to
the event start ("Opens in HH:MM:SS").

.Acceptance criteria
. The icon is detected on `ref/flame-and-fangs-announced.png` and on no other Intel reference.
. Its countdown reads 07:52:41 on that screenshot. The event start is the time of the
  screenshot plus the countdown.
. Without the icon the run works as on a normal day; the pre-event rules (<<FR-5.3>>,
  <<FR-5.4>>) do not apply.
. If the icon is shown but its countdown stays unreadable for 8 s, the run starts no mission,
  claims nothing and ends regularly with "event countdown unreadable".
. The event start is logged once per run.
