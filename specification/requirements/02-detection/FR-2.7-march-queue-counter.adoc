=== FR-2.7 March queue counter

The counter "Marching N/M" top left on the world map tells how many march queues are busy (N)
out of how many there are (M).

.Acceptance criteria
. The counter is read on the world map with and without the popup of a target.
. N and M are read as separate numbers; the "/" between them is never taken for a digit.
. With the marching panel collapsed to the left edge, and on screens without the counter, the
  result is "unreadable", never a guessed number.
. On the reference screenshots of the world map the counter reads what the screens show.
