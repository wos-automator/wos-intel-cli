=== FR-2.7 March queue counter

The counter "Marching N/M" top left on the world map tells how many march queues are busy (N)
out of how many there are (M). It is read without an OCR engine, with the digits of <<FR-2.5>>.

.Acceptance criteria
. The counter is read on the world map with and without the popup of a target.
. `ref/hunt-2-world-queues-busy.png` reads 6/6, `ref/hunt-2-world-no-popup.png` 5/6,
  `ref/world-target-vanished.png` 3/6 and `ref/hunt-2-world-attack.png` 1/6.
. The "/" between the numbers is recognised as the separator, never as a digit.
. With the marching panel collapsed to the left edge (`ref/boss-2-world-no-popup.png`) and on
  the Intel screen the counter is "unreadable", never a guessed number.
