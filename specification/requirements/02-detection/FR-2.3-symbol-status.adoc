=== FR-2.3 Symbol status

For every mission symbol the system reports whether the mission can be started (`AVAILABLE`),
is under way (`RUNNING`) or is finished (`DONE`).

.Acceptance criteria
. A finished mission, shown with a green check mark, is `DONE`.
. A mission under way is `RUNNING`, also when its symbol is drawn dimmed.
. A symbol whose status is uncertain is `RUNNING`, never `AVAILABLE`, so that an uncertain
  mission is never started.
. On the reference screenshots every symbol has the status of SPEC.md §3.5; the boss of
  `ref/intel-boss-running.png` is `RUNNING`.
