=== FR-2.6 Mission type in the dialog and action button in the popup

.Acceptance criteria
. The pin of the mission dialog is classified as one of the four mission types;
  `combat-1-dialog.png` yields `COMBAT`, `hunt-1-dialog.png` yields `HUNT`,
  `boss-1-dialog.png` yields `BOSS`.
. The action button of the world-map popup is searched by template in y 250–700 and classified
  as Explore or Attack; only the upper half with the label is matched.
. A button without template whose median hue (pixels with S > 120 and V > 120 in the region
  x 245–475, y 598–660) lies in 50–75 is classified as Rescue.
. The green hint "You are quite likely to prevail" on the Deploy screen is detected; the red
  "You are not likely to prevail" (`boss-3-deploy.png`) does not count as that hint.
. On the world map without popup the intel pin above the target in the centre is classified
  as one of the four mission types; `hunt-2-world-no-popup.png` yields `HUNT` at ≈ 360,661.
  Under a popup and on the plain world map no pin is reported (<<US-5.3>>).
