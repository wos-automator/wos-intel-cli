[[FR-2.6]]
=== FR-2.6 Mission type in the dialog and action button in the popup

.Acceptance criteria
. The pin of a mission dialog is recognised as one of the four mission types; while a
  notification banner covers it, the type is unknown, never a guess.
. The action button of a world-map popup is recognised as Explore, Attack or Rescue; Rescue
  also before a reference screenshot of it exists (<<NFR-3>>).
. The green hint "You are quite likely to prevail" on the Deploy screen is recognised; the red
  "You are not likely to prevail" is not taken for it.
. On the world map without popup, the intel pin above the target in the centre is recognised
  with its mission type and position; under a popup and on the world map without target no
  pin is reported (<<US-5.3>>).
. On the reference screenshots of mission dialogs, popups, Deploy screens and the world map
  the results are those the screens show.
