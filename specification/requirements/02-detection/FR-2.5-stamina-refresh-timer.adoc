=== FR-2.5 Stamina and refresh timer

The stamina (top right) and the remaining time of "Refreshes In" are read from the Intel screen.

.Acceptance criteria
. The stamina is read as a whole number; the thousands separator is not a digit.
. The refresh timer is read as hours, minutes and seconds.
. Digits that touch each other on the screen are read as separate digits.
. On the reference Intel screens stamina and timer read the values the screens show.
. If a digit cannot be recognised the result is "unreadable", never a guessed number.
