=== FR-2.4 Symbol tier

Every mission symbol is reported with its tier `GOLD > PURPLE > BLUE`, the colour of its disc.

.Acceptance criteria
. On the reference Intel screens every symbol has the tier its disc shows.
. The symbols are listed by tier, highest first, then from top to bottom.
