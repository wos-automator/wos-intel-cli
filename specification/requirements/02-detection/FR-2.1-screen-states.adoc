.Acceptance criteria
. Each of the eighteen reference screenshots yields the state listed for it.
. Button and title templates are matched in colour with `TM_CCOEFF_NORMED` and threshold 0.9;
  the Intel button with 0.85, with and without the red badge.
. `DEPLOY` is recognised by the Deploy button, never by the title alone.
. For every state with a tap target the position of that target is reported.
. A screen that matches nothing is `UNKNOWN`.
