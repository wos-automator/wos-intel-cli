=== FR-2.1 Screen states

Every screenshot is classified as exactly one state.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|State |What the screen shows

|`INTEL` |title "Intel" top left
|`INTEL_CLAIMABLE` |as `INTEL` plus the green "Claim All" button
|`REWARDS` |banner "Rewards"
|`MISSION_DIALOG` |blue "View" button
|`WORLD_TARGET` |popup with an Explore, Attack or Rescue button
|`DEPLOY` |"Deploy" button bottom right
|`SQUAD` |green "Fight" button
|`VICTORY` |"Victory!" and "Tap anywhere to exit"
|`DEFEAT` |"Tap anywhere to exit" without "Victory!"
|`WORLD` |round Intel button on the right, no popup
|`NO_STAMINA`, `NO_TROOPS` |blocking dialogs
|`HINT` |hint bubble "You have enabled Simple Battle Mode …" over any screen
|`QUIT_CONFIRMATION` |dialog "Quit game?" with "Cancel" and "Confirm"
|`SESSION_LOST` |dialog "The account has been logged in on another device." with "Reconnect"
|`UNKNOWN` |none of the above
|===

.Acceptance criteria
. Each reference screenshot in `ref/` is classified as the state of the screen it shows.
. The world map is recognised whether or not its Intel button carries the red badge.
. A hint bubble is recognised as `HINT` whatever screen lies beneath it (<<US-5.1>>,
  BUG-0002).
. For every state with a tap target the position of that target is reported.
. A screen that shows none of the states is `UNKNOWN`.
