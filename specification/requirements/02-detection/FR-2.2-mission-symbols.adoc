=== FR-2.2 Mission symbols

On the Intel screen the mission symbols are found with type and click point.

.Acceptance criteria
. The types are `HUNT` (paw, or the firebeast during Flame and Fangs, <<FR-5.5>>), `RESCUE`
  (tent), `COMBAT` (halberds), `BOSS` (skull).
. The click point of a symbol is the centre of its disc.
. Only the mission area yields symbols: the title, timer and stamina at the top and the level
  bar and "Claim All" at the bottom never do.
. On `ref/intel-x.png`, `-xx.png` and `-xxx.png` exactly the symbols of SPEC.md §3.5 are
  found, each within ±6 px.
