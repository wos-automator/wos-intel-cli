=== FR-2.2 Mission symbols

On the Intel screen the mission symbols are found with type and click point.

.Acceptance criteria
. Search band y 170–1050; white mask HSV (0,0,215)–(180,45,255); `TM_CCOEFF_NORMED` against
  `paw_mask`, `tent_mask`, `axe_mask`, `skull_mask`; threshold 0.75; non-maximum suppression
  within 20 px.
. The types are `HUNT` (paw), `RESCUE` (tent), `COMBAT` (halberds), `BOSS` (skull).
. The click point is the centre of the match (centre of the disc).
. On `ref/intel-x.png`, `-xx.png` and `-xxx.png` exactly the symbols of SPEC.md §3.5 are
  found, each within ±6 px.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.1, §3.2, §3.5; acceptance criterion 1 in §8 +
Effort:: 5 h
