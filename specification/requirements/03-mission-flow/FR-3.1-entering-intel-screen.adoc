=== FR-3.1 Entering the Intel screen

.Acceptance criteria
. On `WORLD` the Intel button is tapped and `INTEL` is awaited.
. On `INTEL` or `INTEL_CLAIMABLE` the run starts directly.
. On any other state the run aborts with a message; nothing is tapped ("no guessing").
