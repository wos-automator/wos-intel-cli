[#FR-3.3]
=== FR-3.3 Mission selection

.Acceptance criteria
. Only `AVAILABLE` symbols are candidates, in the order of <<FR-2.4>>.
. The boss is the exception to that order: it is attacked last, only when no other affordable
  candidate is left, and once (<<FR-3.5>>).
. A mission is only started if the stamina covers its cost (hunt 10, rescue 12, combat 10,
  boss 10); unaffordable missions are skipped.
. The run ends regularly when the stamina is below 10, when no candidate is left, or when
  no remaining candidate is affordable – after running missions have been waited for
  (<<FR-3.7>>). Hunt and boss targets deferred because all march queues are busy do not end
  the run (<<FR-3.9>>).
. If the stamina cannot be read the run continues.
. A symbol is attempted at most once per run.
. If another detected symbol lies closer than 30 px to the click point and further in front
  (larger y of the ground ring), the one in front is processed first; if that one is not a
  candidate, the tap moves to the edge of the disc opposite to it.
