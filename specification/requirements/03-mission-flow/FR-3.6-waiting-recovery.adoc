[[FR-3.6]]
=== FR-3.6 Waiting and recovery

.Acceptance criteria
. After every tap the program waits briefly, then takes a fresh screenshot and determines the
  state; taps are never chained without looking.
. A step waits for its expected state for a limited time, taking fresh screenshots meanwhile;
  then it has failed.
. A hint bubble (`HINT`) is dismissed by a tap on its detected text before the state is
  evaluated, a limited number of times in a row; the running step continues and BACK is never
  pressed on it (<<US-5.1>>).
. After a failed step the run recovers with a limited number of BACK presses, re-detecting
  before each one; BACK is never pressed on `INTEL` or `WORLD`. `REWARDS`, `VICTORY` and
  `DEFEAT` are left by their exit tap.
. If a BACK opens the "Quit game?" confirmation, "Cancel" is tapped and the recovery ends:
  nothing was open any more, so no further BACK is pressed (<<US-5.2>>).
. If the account has been logged in on another device (`SESSION_LOST`), the run waits 60 s
  (configurable) so that the player can look something up, then taps "Reconnect" and waits for
  `INTEL` or `WORLD`. The interrupted step is abandoned without counting as a failure and the
  run starts over with the next iteration (<<US-5.4>>).
. After 3 failed steps in a row (configurable) the run ends with exit code 1.
. The screenshot on which a mission was skipped or a step failed is saved under `debug/`.
