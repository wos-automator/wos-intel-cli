=== FR-3.6 Waiting and recovery

.Acceptance criteria
. After every tap the program waits 1.0 s (configurable), then takes a fresh screenshot and
  determines the state.
. Waiting for an expected state lasts at most 8 s (configurable) with a screenshot every 0.7 s.
. A hint bubble (`HINT`) is dismissed by a tap on its detected text before the state is
  evaluated, at most 3 times in a row; the running step continues and BACK is never pressed on
  it (<<US-5.1>>).
. After a timeout BACK is pressed up to 3 times, re-detecting before each press; BACK is never
  pressed on `INTEL` or `WORLD`. `REWARDS`, `VICTORY` and `DEFEAT` are left by their exit tap.
. If a BACK opens the "Quit game?" confirmation, "Cancel" is tapped and the recovery ends:
  nothing was open any more, so no further BACK is pressed (<<US-5.2>>).
. If the account has been logged in on another device (`SESSION_LOST`), the run waits 60 s
  (configurable) so that the player can look something up, then taps "Reconnect" and waits up
  to 120 s (configurable) for `INTEL` or `WORLD`. The interrupted step is abandoned without
  counting as a failure and the run starts over with the next iteration (<<US-5.4>>).
. After 3 failed steps in a row the run ends with exit code 1.
. The screenshot on which a mission was skipped or a step failed is saved under `debug/`.
