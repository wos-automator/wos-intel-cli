=== FR-3.8 Expert: more missions once a day

Once a day a white sign pointing left with a bust portrait – the expert – appears top left on
the Intel screen. Tapping it yields additional missions.

.Acceptance criteria
. The sign is recognised on `ref/intel-expert-available.png` and on no other reference
  screenshot.
. When no regular mission is left to work on (boss aside) and the sign is visible, it is
  tapped – before the boss (<<FR-3.3>>), before waiting for running missions (<<FR-3.7>>) and
  also when the stamina is below the minimum, so that the daily offer is not lost.
. While a regular mission can still be worked on, the sign is not tapped.
. The sign is tapped at most once per run; afterwards the Intel screen is evaluated again.
. Whenever the sign is visible on the Intel screen this is logged with its position, also when
  it is not tapped.
. A dry run logs the tap instead of sending it.
. Before the Flame and Fangs event the sign is only tapped within 2 h of the start (<<FR-5.4>>).
