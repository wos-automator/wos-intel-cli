[[FR-3.5]]
=== FR-3.5 Boss only once per refresh cycle

.Acceptance criteria
. After a boss attack the boss is locked until the end of the refresh cycle: time of the
  attack plus the remaining time of "Refreshes In" read before the attack.
. If the timer is unreadable the lock lasts a configurable time (default 8 h).
. The lock is stored in `state.json` and survives a restart.
. A locked boss is not a candidate; an expired lock has no effect.
. The boss is locked only once it has been deployed: a boss whose popup does not open is
  tapped again like any other target (<<FR-3.4>>) and stays unlocked if that fails too
  (<<US-5.5>>, BUG-0098).
