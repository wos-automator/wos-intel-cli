=== FR-3.2 Claiming rewards

.Acceptance criteria
. On `INTEL_CLAIMABLE` "Claim All" is tapped at its detected position and `REWARDS` awaited.
. `REWARDS` is left by a tap at 360,180 (above the rewards); then `INTEL` is awaited.
. Rewards are claimed before the next mission is selected.
. Exception: in the hold phase before the Flame and Fangs event nothing is claimed (<<FR-5.3>>).
