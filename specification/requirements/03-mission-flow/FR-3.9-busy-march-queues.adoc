[[FR-3.9]]
=== FR-3.9 Busy march queues

A hunt or boss target sends troops and needs a free march queue; combat and rescue do not.
With all march queues busy the game opens no popup for a hunt or boss target (BUG-0014). The
run then works on the other targets and leaves hunt and boss for later instead of failing or
ending.

.Acceptance criteria
. Every screenshot of the world map with a readable counter (<<FR-2.7>>) updates what the run
  knows about the queues: N ≥ M means busy, N < M means free. An unreadable counter changes
  nothing.
. While the queues are busy, hunt and boss targets are deferred: they are not tapped and do not
  count as attempted. Combat and rescue targets are started in the order of <<FR-3.3>>.
. If the popup of a hunt or boss target does not open after "View" and the counter shows all
  queues busy, the target is not tapped again (no retry of <<FR-3.4>>). The target is deferred,
  the run returns to the Intel screen, and no failure is counted.
. If only deferred targets are left, the run does not end. It waits briefly on the Intel screen
  and then tries the first deferred target again; its world map shows the counter once more.
. While it waits, the run keeps looking at the Intel screen. As soon as "Claim All"
  appears the wait ends: the rewards are claimed (<<FR-3.2>>) and the deferred target is tried
  again at once, because a claimed hunt is a march that has returned. In the hold phase before
  the Flame and Fangs event nothing is claimed and "Claim All" does not end the wait
  (<<FR-5.3>>).
. If the queues are still busy 15 min (configurable) after they were first seen busy, the run
  ends regularly with "all march queues busy", after running missions have been waited for
  (<<FR-3.7>>). A counter that shows a free queue restarts this period.
. The boss is locked for the refresh cycle only when it has been deployed, never when it was
  deferred (<<FR-3.5>>).
. A dry run never waits.
