=== NFR-3 Screens without reference screenshot

Rescue (popup button and what follows), `DEFEAT`, the "not enough stamina" dialog and the
"no troops / march slots" dialog have no reference screenshot yet.

.Acceptance criteria
. The steps are implemented and become active as soon as their screen can be recognised from a
  reference screenshot, without a code change; until then they are skipped without an error.
. `snap` records the missing screens (<<FR-1.3>>).
. Until verified on the device these paths are exercised with `--dry-run` and with tests on
  synthetic screens only.
