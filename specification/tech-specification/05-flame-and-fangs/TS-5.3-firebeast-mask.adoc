[#TS-5.3]
=== TS-5.3 Firebeast mask

Implements <<FR-5.5>>.

* `MASK_TEMPLATES` is a list of (type, mask) pairs, so one type can have several masks;
  `firebeast_mask` is a second `HUNT` mask next to `paw_mask`. `detect_symbols` (<<TS-2.2>>),
  `dialog_type` and `world_pin` iterate over all pairs.
* `tools/cut_templates.py` cuts `firebeast_mask` as the white mask (`symbols.white_lo` –
  `white_hi`) of `ref/flame-and-fangs-missions.png` (406, 255, 454, 303), 48 × 48 px centred on
  the disc, like the other symbol masks. It scores 1.0 at 430,279 and at most 0.61 on every
  other reference (both passes); the other masks find nothing there.
* `templates/flame-and-fangs-firebeast-mission.png` delivered with SPEC.md §3.7 is a colour
  picture of the whole pin at another scale; it stays as the stakeholder's source and is not
  used for matching.
* No dialog or world map screenshot of the firebeast exists yet. `dialog_type` and `world_pin`
  try the firebeast mask at their pin scales, but these are not verified for it; an
  unrecognised dialog leads to the plausibility rule of <<FR-2.2>> (the target is locked for
  the run).
