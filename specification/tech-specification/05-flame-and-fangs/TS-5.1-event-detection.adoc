[[TS-5.1]]
=== TS-5.1 Event icon, countdown and mission expiry

Implements <<FR-5.1>> and <<FR-5.2>>.

* Template `event_icon`: cut by `tools/cut_templates.py` from the creature head of the icon in
  `ref/flame-and-fangs-announced.png` (≈ 595–690 / 1150–1210), without the countdown below
  it. `templates/flame-and-fangs-event-icon.png` delivered with SPEC.md §3.7 is about twice
  the device scale (182 px wide, the icon on the screenshot ≈ 95 px) and has an alpha channel;
  it stays as the stakeholder's source and is not used for matching. Matched by
  `Detector.find` in `regions.event_icon` against `buttons.threshold`: the cut scores 1.0 on
  its reference and at most 0.50 on every other one. It is a required template like
  `expert_sign`, because it is cut from a reference.
* `Detector.find_event_icon(image) -> Match | None`, asked on every evaluation of the Intel
  screen.
* `Detector.read_event_countdown(image) -> int | None`: the six rightmost glyphs of
  `ocr.countdown_region` as HHMMSS, like `read_refresh_seconds`
  (<<TS-2.3>>). The digits are pure white with a dark outline on a light map (V ≈ 220,
  S ≈ 30), which the white mask of `[ocr]` takes in as well; the countdown therefore has its own
  mask `ocr.countdown_lo` / `ocr.countdown_hi`. With it the existing digit templates read
  07:52:41 on `ref/flame-and-fangs-announced.png` with scores ≥ 0.82.
* `Runner._note_event(image)`: on every evaluation of the Intel screen; with the icon and a
  readable countdown it sets `_event_start = now() + countdown` once per run and logs it.
* `Detector.read_expiry_seconds(image) -> int | None`: the six rightmost glyphs of
  `ocr.expiry_region` as HHMMSS, segmented with the dark-text mask `ocr.dark_lo` /
  `ocr.dark_hi`. The line is at y ≈ 860 on the combat and
  hunt dialogs and at y ≈ 817 on the rescue dialog; the region covers both, and the white
  "View" and reward numbers drop out of the dark mask. With the white digit templates alone the
  scores went down to 0.68, so `tools/cut_templates.py` cuts digit templates
  `templates/digits/<digit>_expiry-<a-d>-<index>.png` from the four dialog references (digit
  sources with the colour `dark`). They hold every digit except 6; until a dialog with a 6
  exists, the 6 is classified by the white templates of the shared pool. On
  `ref/boss-1-dialog.png` the region holds no glyph: `None`.
* Same rules as <<TS-2.3>>: below `glyph_threshold` the whole reading is `None`, minutes and
  seconds above 59 give `None`.

Checked by `tests/test_detect.py`: `test_event_icon_and_countdown` (icon and 07:52:41 on
`flame-and-fangs-announced`), `test_no_event_icon_on_a_normal_day`, `test_mission_expiry`
(`combat-1-dialog` 13:51:07, `hunt-1-dialog` 13:48:42, `hunt-1-dialog-banner` 14:59:41,
`rescue-1-dialog-banner` 14:59:19), `test_no_mission_expiry_without_the_line` (`boss-1-dialog`)
and `test_covered_mission_expiry_is_unreadable`; the run with the event by the `test_flow.py`
tests named in <<TS-5.2>>. The `detect` command does not report the event icon, the countdown
or the expiry yet, so they have no acceptance scenario.
