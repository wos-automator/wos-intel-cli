[[TS-4.1]]
=== TS-4.1 Safety by construction

Implements <<FR-4.1>> and <<FR-4.2>>.

* All input goes through `Runner._tap` and `Runner._back`. `_tap` is called with a `Match`
  point, a symbol click point from `choose_target`, or `buttons.rewards_exit`; there is no other
  source of coordinates.
* The set of templates that can be tapped is closed: Intel button, Claim All, expert sign, View,
  Explore/Attack/Rescue, Fight, Deploy, "Tap anywhere to exit". Shop, purchase and diamond
  dialogs match none of them, are `UNKNOWN` and are only ever answered with BACK.
* "Quick Deploy" is never a target: only `btn_fight` is matched on `SQUAD`.
* `Adb` has no swipe/zoom/text method and `Adb.tap` rejects off-screen coordinates
  (<<TS-1.1>>).
