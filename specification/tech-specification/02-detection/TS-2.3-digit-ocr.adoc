[[TS-2.3]]
=== TS-2.3 Digit OCR

Implements <<FR-2.5>> and <<FR-2.7>>.

* `segment_glyphs(image, lo, hi, min_height_ratio, min_height, max_aspect)`: white mask of the
  region, connected components, components lower than `min_height` or lower than
  `min_height_ratio` × the tallest one are dropped (comma, colon dots), the rest is returned
  left to right. A component wider than `glyph_max_aspect` (1.3) × its height consists of
  touching digits (the bold "46" of 02:46:03 is one 35x21 component); it is cut into
  `round(width / typical width)` parts at the thinnest column near each even division.
* `normalize_glyph(glyph)`: scaled to 16 px height keeping the aspect ratio, centred on a
  16x16 canvas. A "1" therefore stays narrow.
* Classification: best `TM_CCOEFF_NORMED` score against all digit templates in
  `templates/digits/<digit>_<source>.png`; below `glyph_threshold` (0.6) the whole reading is
  `None`.
* `read_stamina(image)`: all glyphs of `stamina_region`, at most `max_stamina_digits`.
* `read_refresh_seconds(image)`: the six rightmost glyphs of `timer_region` as HHMMSS; minutes
  and seconds above 59 give `None`. The letters of "Refreshes In" left of them are ignored.
* `read_marches(image) -> tuple[int, int] | None`: the glyphs of `ocr.march_region`
  (185, 195, 250, 232), the "N/M" right of the word "Marching". Exactly one glyph scores below
  `glyph_threshold` against every digit template: the "/" (best score 0.43, the digits ≥ 0.85
  on the references). The glyphs left of it are N, the ones right of it M; both must be
  non-empty and every digit must reach `glyph_threshold`, otherwise `None`. A collapsed panel
  leaves no glyph in the region and gives `None`.

`tools/cut_templates.py` generates the digit templates from `ref/`: stamina (0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7),
refresh timer (0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 9) and the troop and power numbers of the Deploy screen
(0, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9). `ref/intel-boss-running.png` adds 1, 5, 7, 9 in the stamina size and
0, 2, 3, 4, 6 in the timer size. The glyphs for 6 and 8 in the stamina size and for 8 in the
timer size still come from another font size; normalising the height makes them usable.
