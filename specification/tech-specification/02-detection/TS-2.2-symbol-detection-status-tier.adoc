[[TS-2.2]]
=== TS-2.2 Symbol detection, status and tier

Implements <<FR-2.2>>, <<FR-2.3>> and <<FR-2.4>>.

`detect_symbols(image) -> list[Symbol]`:

. HSV image; white mask `inRange(white_lo, white_hi)`; rows outside `search_y` set to 0, so
  the title, timer, stamina, level bar and "Claim All" never yield a symbol.
. Per type: `matchTemplate(mask, <type>_mask, TM_CCOEFF_NORMED)`; repeatedly take the maximum
  while it is ≥ `match_threshold` and blank ±`nms_radius` around it; then keep the best hit
  within `nms_radius` across all types (`_mask_hits`).
. Second pass with the wider mask `dim_white_lo`–`dim_white_hi`: a
  running mission is drawn half transparent and its symbol is too dark for the first mask
  (running boss of `ref/intel-boss-running.png`: 0.51 with the first mask, 0.82 with the
  second; other types ≤ 0.46). Hits within `nms_radius` of a first-pass hit are dropped; the
  remaining ones are treated like first-pass hits. The pass that found a symbol says nothing
  about its status: on the device a vivid, partly covered pin (saturation 224) was found by
  the second pass only, with a score of 0.76. On the other Intel references this pass adds
  nothing.
. Disc statistics: pixels within `disc_radius` of the centre that are not white
  (`disc_white_lo`–`disc_white_hi`); median saturation and median hue.
. Check marks: connected components of the green mask (`check_lo`–`check_hi`) inside the
  search band with an area between `check_min_area` and `check_max_area`.
. Status: `DONE` if a check mark centroid lies within `check_tolerance` of centre +
  `check_offset`; else `AVAILABLE` if saturation ≥ `available_min_saturation`; else `RUNNING`.
  Saturations from `running_max_saturation` up to `available_min_saturation` are uncertain:
  they count as `RUNNING` (logged at debug level), so an uncertain mission is never started.
. Tier: nearest of `hue_gold`, `hue_blue`, `hue_purple` by `hue_distance` (circular, 0–179).
  The tier of a greyed-out `RUNNING` symbol is not reliable and is never used.
. Result sorted by `(-tier, y, x)`.

This is the algorithm of `tools/prototype_detect.py`; its output in SPEC.md §3.5 is the expected
result, checked by `tests/test_detect.py` and by the acceptance scenarios of <<FR-2.2>>,
<<FR-2.3>> and <<FR-2.4>>. The keys are those of `[symbols]` in `config.toml`.
