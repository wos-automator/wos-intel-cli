[[TS-2.4]]
=== TS-2.4 Dialog type, action button, prevail hint

Implements <<FR-2.6>>.

* `dialog_type(image) -> (MissionType, score) | None`: white mask of `dialog_pin_region`; each
  symbol mask scaled by `dialog_pin_scales` (the pin in the dialog is smaller than on the
  Intel screen) and matched; best type if its score reaches `dialog_pin_threshold`. Measured:
  paw and axe pins at ≈ 359,367 score 0.93–0.94 at scale 0.75; the boss shield of
  `ref/boss-1-dialog.png` sits lower at ≈ 356,430 and scores 0.85 at scale 0.60; other types
  ≤ 0.58. A banner over the pin leaves every type below the threshold, so the result is
  `None`.
* `world_pin(image) -> (MissionType, Match) | None`: the same mask matching (shared helper
  `_pin`) in `world_pin_region` with `world_pin_scales` and `world_pin_threshold`. Measured on
  the failure screenshots of 2026-10-17: paw pins at 360,659–662 score 0.82–0.93, skull pins
  at 356,661 score 0.75–0.84; a map without target (`failure-0118-WORLD.png`) scores ≤ 0.70,
  the popup screenshots ≤ 0.58 (<<US-5.3>>).
* The boss dialog is lower than the hunt and combat dialogs: its "View" button is at 360,870
  instead of 360,932, so `regions.btn_view` reaches down to both.
* `find_action_button(image) -> (Action, Match) | None`: `btn_explore_top`, `btn_attack` and
  the optional `btn_rescue` in `buttons.action_region`, best score wins; only the upper half of
  a button, with its label, is matched. Without a template hit: in `buttons.color_region` the
  pixels reaching `color_min_saturation` and `color_min_value` must cover at least
  `color_min_fraction`, and their median hue must lie in `hue_rescue` → `RESCUE` with the
  region centre as tap point. Measured hue: Explore 105, Attack 12.
* `likely_to_prevail(image)`: `text_likely_prevail` (colour template, so a differently
  coloured hint does not match).
* `annotate(image, screen, symbols)`: copy of the screenshot with state, matches and symbols.
