[#US-6.2]
=== US-6.2 Read the mission expiry

Status:: Open +
Effort:: 4 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-5.1>>, <<FR-5.2>>

As the operator, I want the run to read how long a mission stays available, so that it can
decide whether the mission belongs before or into the event.

.Acceptance criteria
. Digit templates for the dark expiry text are cut by `tools/cut_templates.py` from the four
  dialog references.
. The expiries of <<FR-5.2>> are read; the boss dialog gives none.
. A dialog with a covered or cut-off expiry gives "unreadable".

.Test criteria
* Unit test (`tests/test_detect.py`): parametrised over the dialog references; a synthetic
  dialog with the expiry line painted over.
