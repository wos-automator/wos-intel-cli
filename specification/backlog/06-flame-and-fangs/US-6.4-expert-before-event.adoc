[[US-6.4]]
=== US-6.4 Expert only shortly before the event

Status:: Done (0.1.1) – verified on the fake game only +
Effort:: 1 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-5.2>>, <<FR-5.4>>, <<FR-3.8>>

As the player, I want the expert to hand out its missions only shortly before the event, so
that they are still available when the event starts.

.Acceptance criteria
. With a countdown above 2 h the sign is logged but not tapped.
. With a countdown of 2 h or less it is tapped once no mission of the current phase is left.

.Test criteria
* Component test (`tests/test_flow.py`): both criteria, on an Intel reference with the expert
  sign and the event icon pasted in.
