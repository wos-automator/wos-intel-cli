=== US-2.1 Screen states

Status:: Done (0.1.0) +
Effort:: 6 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-2.1>>, <<FR-2.1>>

As the run loop, I want every screenshot classified as one state with its tap targets, so that
no tap is ever sent blindly.

.Acceptance criteria
. All eighteen reference screenshots yield their state; the lower boss dialog has its "View"
  button at 360,870.
. A world map without popup is `WORLD`; a result screen without "Victory!" is `DEFEAT`; a
  blank image is `UNKNOWN`.
. The tap targets lie within ±6 px of the positions in SPEC.md §2 (Claim All 360,1128; View
  360,933; Fight 528,1201; exit 360,1140; Deploy 548,1215; Intel button 663,862).
. The Intel button is found with and without the red badge.
. A missing optional template never matches.

.Test criteria
* Unit test (pytest, `tests/test_detect.py`): parametrised over the reference screenshots and
  the synthetic screens.
