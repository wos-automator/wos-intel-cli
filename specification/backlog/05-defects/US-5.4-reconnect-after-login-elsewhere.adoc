=== US-5.4 BUG-0095a Account logged in on another device

Status:: Open +
Effort:: 3 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-3.5>>, <<FR-3.6>>, <<FR-4.1>>

As the player, I want the run to give me a minute when I log in on another device and to
reclaim the session afterwards, so that a quick look on my phone neither stops the run nor is
interrupted at once.

Defect:: A "Tips" dialog covers the world map: "The account has been logged in on another
device. Please sign in again on this device." with the buttons "Contact Us" and "Reconnect".
The dialog is not detected; every step fails until the run ends. Screenshots:
`failure-0095-UNKNOWN.png`, `failure-0099-UNKNOWN.png`, `failure-0103-UNKNOWN.png`
(byte-identical).

.Acceptance criteria
. The dialog is detected by its text on `ref/dialog-logged-in-elsewhere.png`; its "Reconnect"
  button is reported as tap target.
. Whenever the dialog is seen, the run waits 60 s (configurable), then taps "Reconnect".
  "Contact Us" is never tapped.
. After the reconnect the run waits up to 120 s (configurable) for the Intel screen or the
  world map and starts the next iteration; the interrupted step costs no failure.

.Test criteria
* Unit test (`tests/test_detect.py`): state and "Reconnect" position on the reference
  screenshot.
* Component test (`tests/test_flow.py`, fake clock): the tap on "Reconnect" comes 60 s after
  the dialog appeared, the run then finishes regularly.
