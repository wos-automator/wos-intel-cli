=== US-5.7 Waiting after the boss does not end

Status:: Done (0.1.1) – verified on the fake game only +
Effort:: 2 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-3.1>>, <<FR-3.7>>

As the operator, I want the run to stop waiting when there is nothing left to wait for, so that
it does not sit idle while missions are available or after a lost boss.

Defect:: Reported by the operator from a device run, without a failure screenshot (the run did
not fail). After the boss was attacked the run waited the full 900 s, although missions were
available and the boss was finished or lost: the wait only looked for "Claim All", and a lost
boss pays nothing. A first fix that stopped as soon as no symbol was running ended the wait
after 30 s while a hunt was still running: its pin was hidden behind the boss.

.Acceptance criteria
. The wait lasts at most 5 min (`completion_timeout` 300 s).
. A mission that becomes workable while waiting ends the wait and is started.
. With only the boss started since the last claim, the wait ends as soon as no symbol is
  `RUNNING` and nothing can be claimed.
. A started hunt, combat or rescue is waited for until "Claim All" appears or the timeout,
  even if no symbol is shown as `RUNNING`.

.Test criteria
* Component test (`tests/test_flow.py`): the running boss vanishes after 40 s → the run ends
  at about 40 s; missions appear after 20 s → they are started; a started mission whose pin is
  not shown → the run waits the full 300 s.
* Integration test (`tests/test_config_state.py`): default `completion_timeout` 300 s.
