[[US-3.7]]
=== US-3.7 Use the expert for more missions

Status:: Done (0.1.0) – what follows the tap is not verified on the device, see <<US-4.3>> +
Effort:: 3 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-3.1>>, <<FR-3.8>>, <<FR-3.3>>

As the operator, I want the daily expert offer used as soon as the regular missions are worked
off, so that the additional missions are never lost.

.Acceptance criteria
. The sign is found on `intel-expert-available.png` at 59,210 (±6 px) and on no Intel
  reference without it.
. With only a running boss and the sign on the screen, the first tap is the sign.
. With regular missions and the sign: all regular missions first, then the sign, and the boss
  only afterwards.
. On `intel-expert-available.png` itself (eight available missions) a dry run plans the golden
  rescue, not the sign.
. With stamina 9 the sign is still tapped; the run then ends with `OUT_OF_STAMINA`.
. If the sign stays visible after the tap it is not tapped again in this run.
. A dry run logs `would tap 59,210 (expert_sign)` and sends nothing.
. A visible sign is always logged as `expert sign at 59,210`, also while missions are left.

.Test criteria
* Unit test (pytest, `tests/test_detect.py`): sign found / not found; the new reference reads
  state, stamina 2165, timer 00:54:43 and eight symbols.
* Component test (pytest + fake game, `tests/test_flow.py`): one test per flow criterion, on
  Intel references with the sign pasted in.
