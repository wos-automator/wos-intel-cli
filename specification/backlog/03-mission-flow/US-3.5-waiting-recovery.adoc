[[US-3.5]]
=== US-3.5 Waiting and recovery

Status:: Done (0.1.0) +
Effort:: 3 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-3.5>>, <<FR-3.6>>

As the operator, I want the run to leave unexpected screens carefully and to stop when it
keeps failing, so that it never loops or clicks around.

.Acceptance criteria
. A step polls every 0.7 s for 8 s (13 screenshots) before it fails.
. An unexpected screen is left with BACK; nothing is tapped on it.
. BACK is never pressed on `INTEL` or `WORLD`.
. Three failed steps in a row end the run with `TOO_MANY_FAILURES`.
. The screenshot of a skipped mission is saved as `debug/failure-<step>-<STATE>.png`.

.Test criteria
* Component test (pytest + fake game with a fake clock, `tests/test_flow.py`): screenshot
  count, BACK on an unknown screen, three failures, saved failure screenshots.
