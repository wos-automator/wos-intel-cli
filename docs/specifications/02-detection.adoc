== Detection: Screens, Symbols, Stamina

[#FR-2.1]
=== FR-2.1 Screen states

Every screenshot is classified as exactly one state.

[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
|State |Feature |Reference

|`INTEL` |title "Intel" top left |`ref/intel-x.png`, `ref/intel-xx.png`
|`INTEL_CLAIMABLE` |as `INTEL` plus the green "Claim All" button |`ref/intel-xxx.png`
|`REWARDS` |banner "Rewards" |`ref/intel-xxxx.png`
|`MISSION_DIALOG` |blue "View" button |`ref/combat-1-dialog.png`, `ref/hunt-1-dialog.png`, `ref/boss-1-dialog.png`
|`WORLD_TARGET` |popup with an Explore, Attack or Rescue button |`ref/combat-2-world-explore.png`, `ref/hunt-2-world-attack.png`
|`DEPLOY` |"Deploy" button bottom right |`ref/hunt-3-deploy.png`
|`SQUAD` |green "Fight" button |`ref/combat-3-squad-fight.png`
|`VICTORY` |"Victory!" and "Tap anywhere to exit" |`ref/combat-4-victory.png`
|`DEFEAT` |"Tap anywhere to exit" without "Victory!" |none yet
|`WORLD` |round Intel button on the right, no popup |visible in `ref/combat-2-world-explore.png`
|`NO_STAMINA`, `NO_TROOPS` |blocking dialogs |none yet
|`UNKNOWN` |none of the above |–
|===

.Acceptance criteria
. Each of the fourteen reference screenshots yields the state listed for it.
. Button and title templates are matched in colour with `TM_CCOEFF_NORMED` and threshold 0.9;
  the Intel button with 0.85, with and without the red badge.
. `DEPLOY` is recognised by the Deploy button, never by the title alone.
. For every state with a tap target the position of that target is reported.
. A screen that matches nothing is `UNKNOWN`.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §2, §8 (Templates, cross-check) +
Effort:: 6 h

[#FR-2.2]
=== FR-2.2 Mission symbols

On the Intel screen the mission symbols are found with type and click point.

.Acceptance criteria
. Search band y 170–1050; white mask HSV (0,0,215)–(180,45,255); `TM_CCOEFF_NORMED` against
  `paw_mask`, `tent_mask`, `axe_mask`, `skull_mask`; threshold 0.75; non-maximum suppression
  within 20 px.
. The types are `HUNT` (paw), `RESCUE` (tent), `COMBAT` (halberds), `BOSS` (skull).
. The click point is the centre of the match (centre of the disc).
. On `ref/intel-x.png`, `-xx.png` and `-xxx.png` exactly the symbols of SPEC.md §3.5 are
  found, each within ±6 px.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.1, §3.2, §3.5; acceptance criterion 1 in §8 +
Effort:: 5 h

[#FR-2.3]
=== FR-2.3 Symbol status

.Acceptance criteria
. `DONE`: a green component (HSV 45–75 / S ≥ 120 / V ≥ 120, area 40–600 px) lies within
  14 px of the point +24/−24 px from the disc centre.
. `AVAILABLE`: not `DONE` and the median saturation of the non-white disc pixels is ≥ 150.
. `RUNNING`: everything else; values from 130 to 149 are treated as `RUNNING` so that an
  uncertain symbol is never started.
. On the reference screenshots: the tent is `RUNNING` in `intel-xx.png` (saturation 38) and
  `DONE` in `intel-xxx.png`; the golden paw at 564,737 is `DONE` in `intel-xxx.png`.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.4 +
Effort:: 3 h

[#FR-2.4]
=== FR-2.4 Symbol tier

The tier `GOLD > PURPLE > BLUE` follows from the hue of the disc.

.Acceptance criteria
. The median hue of the non-white disc pixels is assigned to the nearest of gold (16),
  blue (109) and purple (143) on the circular hue scale.
. On `ref/intel-xxx.png`: golden paw and boss are `GOLD`; tent, the axe at 340,378 and the paw
  at 275,677 are `PURPLE`; the remaining three are `BLUE`.
. The symbol list is sorted by tier (highest first), then from top to bottom.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.3 +
Effort:: 2 h

[#FR-2.5]
=== FR-2.5 Stamina and refresh timer

Stamina (top right) and the remaining time of "Refreshes In" are read without an OCR engine.

.Acceptance criteria
. Digits are recognised by matching single glyphs against digit templates; the comma is a
  thousands separator and is ignored.
. `ref/intel-x.png` reads 2047, `intel-xx.png` and `intel-xxx.png` read 2035.
. The refresh timer of the three Intel screenshots reads 06:59:27, 06:59:10 and 06:59:05.
. If a glyph cannot be classified the result is "unreadable", never a guessed number.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §4, §5 (BOSS, refresh cycle) +
Effort:: 5 h

[#FR-2.6]
=== FR-2.6 Mission type in the dialog and action button in the popup

.Acceptance criteria
. The pin of the mission dialog is classified as one of the four mission types;
  `combat-1-dialog.png` yields `COMBAT`, `hunt-1-dialog.png` yields `HUNT`,
  `boss-1-dialog.png` yields `BOSS`.
. The action button of the world-map popup is searched by template in y 250–700 and classified
  as Explore or Attack; only the upper half with the label is matched.
. A button without template whose median hue (pixels with S > 120 and V > 120 in the region
  x 245–475, y 598–660) lies in 50–75 is classified as Rescue.
. The green hint "You are quite likely to prevail" on the Deploy screen is detected; the red
  "You are not likely to prevail" (`boss-3-deploy.png`) does not count as that hint.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §2 (Rescue without a reference image), §3.5 step 6, §5 +
Effort:: 4 h
