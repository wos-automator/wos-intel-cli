== Safety and Quality

[#FR-4.1]
=== FR-4.1 Taps only on detected targets

.Acceptance criteria
. Every tap goes to a position reported by the detection for the current screenshot: a
  template match or a symbol click point.
. The only fixed tap is the documented exit of `REWARDS` at 360,180.
. Shop, purchase, offer and diamond dialogs are never confirmed: a screen that is not one of
  the known states is answered with BACK only.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §6 +
Effort:: 1 h

[#FR-4.2]
=== FR-4.2 The map is never moved

.Acceptance criteria
. The program sends no swipe, drag, zoom or text input.
. The program sends no mouse or keyboard input through macOS.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §1 (macOS input), §6 +
Effort:: 0.5 h

[#FR-4.3]
=== FR-4.3 Upper limit of missions

.Acceptance criteria
. A run starts at most 30 missions (configurable) and then ends regularly.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §6 +
Effort:: 0.5 h

[#NFR-1]
=== NFR-1 Technology

.Acceptance criteria
. Python 3.11 or newer.
. Runtime dependencies are `opencv-python` and `numpy` only; nothing that needs a server and
  no OCR engine.
. Project structure as in SPEC.md §8: `wos_intel/` with `__main__.py`, `adb.py`, `detect.py`,
  `flow.py`, `state.py`; `templates/`, `ref/`, `tests/`, `config.toml`.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §1, §8 +
Effort:: 1 h

[#NFR-2]
=== NFR-2 Quality gate

.Acceptance criteria
. One command (`uv run poe check`) runs lint, format check, type check, the tests and the
  validation of the specification documents; it must pass before a change is done.
. The specification validator checks includes, unique anchors, resolvable references and the
  traceability of technical specifications and user stories.
. The detection tests use the reference screenshots in `ref/` with the expected values of
  SPEC.md §3.5.

Stakeholder need:: AGENTS.md (Definition of Done, Reference Traceability), SPEC.md §8 +
Effort:: 3 h

[#NFR-3]
=== NFR-3 Screens without reference screenshot

Rescue (popup button and what follows), `DEFEAT`, the "not enough stamina" dialog and the
"no troops / march slots" dialog have no reference screenshot yet.

.Acceptance criteria
. The steps are implemented and work as soon as the template exists: `btn_rescue.png`,
  `dialog_no_stamina.png`, `dialog_no_troops.png` in `templates/` are used when present and
  ignored when absent.
. `snap` records the missing screens (<<FR-1.3>>).
. Until verified on the device these paths are exercised with `--dry-run` and with tests on
  synthetic screens only.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §2 ("Still open") +
Effort:: 2 h
