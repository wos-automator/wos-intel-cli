== Mission Flow

[#FR-3.1]
=== FR-3.1 Entering the Intel screen

.Acceptance criteria
. On `WORLD` the Intel button is tapped and `INTEL` is awaited.
. On `INTEL` or `INTEL_CLAIMABLE` the run starts directly.
. On any other state the run aborts with a message; nothing is tapped ("no guessing").

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §5 (start) +
Effort:: 1 h

[#FR-3.2]
=== FR-3.2 Claiming rewards

.Acceptance criteria
. On `INTEL_CLAIMABLE` "Claim All" is tapped at its detected position and `REWARDS` awaited.
. `REWARDS` is left by a tap at 360,180 (above the rewards); then `INTEL` is awaited.
. Rewards are claimed before the next mission is selected.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §5 (loop), acceptance criterion 3 in §8 +
Effort:: 1 h

[#FR-3.3]
=== FR-3.3 Mission selection

.Acceptance criteria
. Only `AVAILABLE` symbols are candidates, in the order of <<FR-2.4>>.
. The boss is the exception to that order: it is attacked last, only when no other affordable
  candidate is left, and once (<<FR-3.5>>).
. A mission is only started if the stamina covers its cost (hunt 10, rescue 12, combat 10,
  boss 10); unaffordable missions are skipped.
. The run ends regularly when the stamina is below 10, when no candidate is left, or when
  no remaining candidate is affordable – after running missions have been waited for
  (<<FR-3.7>>).
. If the stamina cannot be read the run continues.
. A symbol is attempted at most once per run.
. If another detected symbol lies closer than 30 px to the click point and further in front
  (larger y of the ground ring), the one in front is processed first; if that one is not a
  candidate, the tap moves to the edge of the disc opposite to it.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.3, §3.5 step 5, §4, §5 +
Effort:: 4 h

[#FR-3.4]
=== FR-3.4 Starting a mission

.Acceptance criteria
. Tap on the symbol → `MISSION_DIALOG`. The dialog must show the expected mission type
  (<<FR-2.6>>); otherwise BACK and the symbol is skipped for this run.
. Tap "View" → `WORLD_TARGET`. The popup must offer the action of the mission type
  (combat: Explore, hunt and boss: Attack, rescue: Rescue); otherwise BACK and skip.
. Combat: tap the action → `SQUAD` → tap "Fight" (never "Quick Deploy") → `VICTORY` or `DEFEAT`
  within 60 s → tap "Tap anywhere to exit" → `WORLD`.
. Hunt: tap the action → `DEPLOY`. Only if "You are quite likely to prevail" is shown:
  tap "Deploy" → `WORLD`; otherwise BACK and skip. Troops and heroes are never changed.
. Boss: tap the action → `DEPLOY` → tap "Deploy" → `WORLD`, also when the game shows "You are
  not likely to prevail": the boss is attacked once per cycle whatever the odds, and the
  missing hint is logged as a warning. Troops and heroes are never changed.
. Rescue: tap the action → `WORLD`; there is no deploy step.
. After each mission the Intel button on `WORLD` is tapped and `INTEL` awaited.
. A "not enough stamina" dialog ends the run regularly; so does a "no troops / march slots"
  dialog.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.2, §5 +
Effort:: 6 h

[#FR-3.5]
=== FR-3.5 Boss only once per refresh cycle

.Acceptance criteria
. After a boss attack the boss is locked until the end of the refresh cycle: time of the
  attack plus the remaining time of "Refreshes In" read before the attack.
. If the timer is unreadable the lock lasts a configurable time (default 8 h).
. The lock is stored in `state.json` and survives a restart.
. A locked boss is not a candidate; an expired lock has no effect.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §3.2 (BOSS), §5 (BOSS, state.json) +
Effort:: 3 h

[#FR-3.6]
=== FR-3.6 Waiting and recovery

.Acceptance criteria
. After every tap the program waits 1.0 s (configurable), then takes a fresh screenshot and
  determines the state.
. Waiting for an expected state lasts at most 8 s (configurable) with a screenshot every 0.7 s.
. After a timeout BACK is pressed up to 3 times, re-detecting before each press; BACK is never
  pressed on `INTEL` or `WORLD`. `REWARDS`, `VICTORY` and `DEFEAT` are left by their exit tap.
. After 3 failed steps in a row the run ends with exit code 1.
. The screenshot on which a mission was skipped or a step failed is saved under `debug/`.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §2 (state detection), §5 (expect X) +
Effort:: 3 h

[#FR-3.7]
=== FR-3.7 Waiting for running missions and claiming them

The run does not end while missions are running; the player does not have to claim by hand.

.Acceptance criteria
. When nothing is left to start, and a symbol is shown as `RUNNING` or a mission was started in
  this run since the last claim, the program stays on the Intel screen and takes a screenshot
  every 10 s (configurable).
. As soon as "Claim All" appears the rewards are claimed (<<FR-3.2>>) and the Intel screen is
  evaluated again, so further running missions are waited for as well.
. The wait lasts at most 15 min (configurable); then the run ends regularly and the log says
  that rewards were not claimed.
. Without running missions the run ends immediately.
. A dry run only logs that it would wait.

Stakeholder need:: SPEC.md §5 (loop, End of the run), acceptance criterion 3 in §8 +
Effort:: 3 h
